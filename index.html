WristNav Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

WristNav is an Android app with a companion app for Huawei watches. It shows Google Maps directions, ride and food-delivery updates, phone alerts and YouTube Music controls on your watch. This policy explains what data WristNav uses, where that data goes, and the choices you have.

In short: WristNav has no servers, no user accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything it reads stays on your phone, except what it sends to your own watch, and the YouTube requests it makes to Google on your behalf.

WristNav does not use your location, contacts, messages, photos, camera or microphone.

Google and YouTube data

If you choose to connect YouTube on the Music page, WristNav asks Google for read-only access to your YouTube account (the youtube.readonly permission). It uses this only to read the playlists you created and the songs in them, so your watch can list them and start them in YouTube Music. WristNav never changes anything in your YouTube account.

  • The access is handled by Google Play services on your phone. WristNav does not see or store your Google password.
  • Your playlist names are kept on your phone so the watch can show them. Song lists are kept in memory for a few minutes while you browse them on the watch.
  • This data is never sent to the developer, sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any model.
  • You can remove the access at any time with Disconnect YouTube on WristNav's Music page, or in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting also deletes the stored playlist names.

WristNav uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

WristNav's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your Huawei watch

WristNav sends directions, alerts, song titles and playlist names from your phone to the WristNav app on your own Huawei watch. This happens over Bluetooth through Huawei's Wear Engine and the Huawei Health app, which you install and control. Huawei's handling of data in Huawei Health is described in Huawei's own privacy statement. Turn and ride alerts may also be shown as phone notifications, which Huawei Health forwards to your watch if you allow it.

Data stored on your phone

  • Your WristNav settings and your list of YouTube playlist names.
  • If you turn on Record Google Maps' notifications (a troubleshooting tool), the text of Google Maps' navigation notifications is saved to a file on your phone until you clear it. It is only shared if you choose to share it yourself.

Uninstalling WristNav deletes all of this.

Sharing

WristNav does not sell, rent or share your data. Apart from the requests to Google described above and the messages to your own watch, WristNav does not send data anywhere.

Children

WristNav is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
